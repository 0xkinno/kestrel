# Image Prompts (Section 5 of the build spec)

Per the build spec, Kestrel avoids SVG illustration and generic AI-art imagery. These four prompts were drafted as the brief for sourcing commissioned-feeling photography; in this build they were used to search and select real open-license photography directly (via the Unsplash API) rather than generating images, per the spec's own "faster/safer alternative" allowance. See `ATTRIBUTIONS.md` for exactly which photo was used for each and its attribution.

**Style brief prepended to every search:**
"Documentary/editorial photography style, natural light, East African landscape context, muted warm tonal grading matching a palette of paper-white, ink-black, and ochre accent. No text overlay. No illustration style. No 3D render look. Should look like it was commissioned for a print magazine (Monocle-style), not generated for a tech pitch deck."

1. **Hero:** Wide horizon shot, East African savanna/farmland under a shifting sky (storm approaching or dry-season haze) - visually sets up "watching the horizon."
2. **Section break - the kestrel:** A kestrel in flight/hovering, shot naturalistically, not stylized.
3. **Section break - community:** Community/agricultural context shot (farmers, herders, or a small East African town) - respectful, dignified, not poverty-tourism framing. Chosen photo: an everyday market scene rather than posed portraiture, to avoid exoticizing framing.
4. **Data section backdrop:** Aerial/satellite-style landscape shot for the map section backdrop.
